## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is a single-player role-playing business simulation developed and published by Hovgaard Games. It was fully released on August 28, 2026, after entering Early Access on March 10, 2023. Set in a stylized New York City, the game combines life simulation, management, economics, time management, and open-world exploration.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Big Ambitions**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life controls for Big Ambitions. It can adjust personal finances, maintain player needs, enhance skills, manage inventory quantities, and control the passage of time while preserving the game's core business, logistics, property, staffing, and exploration systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with business layouts, explore New York City, test logistics and staffing systems, speed up progression, or reduce routine survival and financial management without changing the game's main business simulation.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Big Ambitions uses an isometric third-person view with point-and-click character movement. Vehicles are driven directly through the city, allowing the player to travel between homes, businesses, suppliers, vehicle dealers, warehouses, and other locations. Time-control options can make long trips and daily errands easier to manage.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory management is central to operating businesses. Products and supplies can be purchased, transported, stored, and placed in shops, restaurants, offices, warehouses, and factories. The inventory option helps maintain carried quantities while setting up locations or testing supply chains.

### World & Environment

The game presents a navigable New York City where buildings can be entered, rented, purchased, furnished, and used for personal or commercial purposes. Players can establish businesses, hire employees, acquire vehicles, use warehouses, import goods, invest in real estate, and expand from a small operation into a larger corporate network.

### Combat & Weapons

Big Ambitions has no combat gameplay. Progress is based on employment, personal needs, business planning, pricing, inventory, staffing, logistics, property management, finances, and investments rather than fighting.
